I have been awarded a nice PhD fellowship
PhD Fellowship
Adrià Mitjavila on June 15, 2022

I am very happy! I have recently been awarded with the INPhINIT Incoming PhD fellowship, a prestigious fellowship awarded by LaCaixa.

It has been 6 months of preparing documentation, writing letters and passing distinct selection processes, including an interview, which granted me 3 years of fellowship to pursue my PhD.

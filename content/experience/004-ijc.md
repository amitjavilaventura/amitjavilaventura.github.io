Bioinformatician & master's thesis student</p>

---

Under the supervision of Dr. Tanya Vavouri, I am studying the PIWI-interacting RNAs (piRNAs), and the evolution and conservation of piRNA-producing loci. 

In January 2022, I completed my master's thesis (MSc in Bioinformatics and Biostatistics, UOC) with honors, in which I analyzed the piRNA variation across closely related mouse species and the factors influencing this variation. 

Currently, I am finishing my PhD thesis in the evolution and conservation of piRNA clusters in mouse. 

A part of this thesis focusses on the comparison of the piRNA production and piRNA-producing loci of distinct inbred and outbred mouse strains, finding that piRNA variation is mostly due to genetic variation, including polymorphisms in endogenous retroviruses (ERVs), particularly intracisternal A particles (IAPs), affecting emergence and expression of piRNA-producing loci.

---

<ins>Tasks:</ins>
